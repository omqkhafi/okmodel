# OKM1707

RLS role

## What happened

A scoped call under rls ran as a role that owns the tenant tables, is a superuser, or has BYPASSRLS. The reason field says which. The message names the endpoint.

## Fix

Connect as a role that is not the table owner, a superuser, or BYPASSRLS. okm seed under rlsTenancy runs as the application role. The owner gets this error.
