`table()` records `file.ts:line` through one stack-reading helper (`undefined` when the runtime gives no stack, and on paths without a `/`, so Windows drive paths lose the location, which is accepted); the location sits on the catalog object and model, `inspect()` copies it onto catalog rules, the duplicate-table error (OKM1023) carries it, and `okm` prints that message; the serialised catalog and its hash leave it out, so a catalog loaded from the document has no source. `okmodel/safety` is a registry plus `safety.property` and loads only when imported; the planner calls it through a one-slot hook that does nothing while no rule is registered. Scope kept small on purpose: the write planner does not call the hook yet (the step that records write contributions wires it), and a SQL-name clash, an emitted-type clash and an unknown foreign-key table do not append a location (adding them cost 183 minified bytes). P21 took 880 minified and 350 gzip in the startup graph (83,559 / 27,546). The cap stays 91,000 / 30,000, so P22 to P29 (eight steps) share 7,441 minified and 2,454 gzip, about 930 and 306 each; gzip is the binding number. From P22 a step reports its estimate of both before building and stops if it expects more than about 900 minified or 300 gzip in the startup graph. Found in P21
