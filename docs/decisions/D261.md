# D261 — `minPostgres` is a major from 15 through 18

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

`schema({ minPostgres: 17 })` is the floor. Omitted means 15 and stores nothing, so a server below 15 is still OKM1803. `requires: { postgres: ">=17" }` is the same stored `>=N` and is deprecated. Both keys together is OKM1060 and names both. Any other value, including a major below 15 or above 18, is OKM1060 (`minPostgres: 15-18.`). The floor is not a catalog object, so the catalog hash does not change. `uuidv7()` needs 18 (`UUIDV7_POSTGRES`). Schema build, apply, and connect read one parser (`parsePostgresFloor`). The schema-build refusal is `uuidv7() needs PostgreSQL 18 or higher. minPostgres: 18.` Connect sentences stay on the connect path. Measured app startup 90,899 / 29,465 (1 under 90,900 / 30,170).
