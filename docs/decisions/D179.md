# D179 — `interval` and `timetz` read-back: fix in P28 under its own stop, else known limit

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

Postgres sends `01:30:00` and `01:02:03+03`, the decoders take only an ISO-8601 duration and a `±HH:MM` offset, so a read that selects such a column fails with OKM1210; writes work. Decision: P28 carries a small second commit that makes both decoders accept what Postgres sends, with a real-Postgres round-trip test. It has its own stop line: if it moves the featureless app by more than +30 / +15 on top of presets, or needs a design, the commit is dropped, the entry stays in known limits for 0.2, and the step reports the numbers. No second design on its own. Found in P27b
