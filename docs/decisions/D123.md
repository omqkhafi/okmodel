# D123 — Contract gating over the whole registry

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

A shared `contract` step runs only when every registered tenant has the migration. Any partial scope (`--canary`, `--class`, `--target` subset) skips contract steps and reports "contract pending (N tenants not at this migration)". Amends D106. Reason: M0-61, M0-62 showed canary and `--class shared` ran the shared contract too early
