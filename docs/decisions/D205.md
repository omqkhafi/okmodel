# D205 — P64 proves the topology on streaming replication

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

The CI compose topology (one primary, `replica-a`, `replica-b`) already existed. P64 adds the conformance file and the harness helpers the tests need: an advisory lock around replay pauses, stop and start of one replica container, and a TCP delay proxy. `tests/topology-conformance.test.ts` is discovered by `scripts/postgres-suite.ts` and is not in `POSTGRES_EXCLUSIONS`. Named tests: `routing.auto` (configured replicas take reads with no option), `routing.classes` (writes, `batch`, locking reads, advisory locks, `tx()`, and every statement inside a transaction stay on the primary, asserted by endpoint), `routing.strict` (paused replay and both replicas stopped are OKM1843 for `route: "replica"` and `using("replica")`; `fallback: "error"` on an automatic read is OKM1844), `pool.separation` (one pool per endpoint, OKM1846 stays on that pool, `reserve()` on postgres.js and PGlite), `tx.affinity` (one primary backend for the transaction, its savepoint, and its batch), `consistency.position` (the write is visible, or the read uses the primary). Commit position: `pg_current_wal_insert_lsn()` after commit is past the in-transaction insert LSN and past the in-transaction `pg_current_wal_lsn()`, with `synchronous_commit` on and off, for one write, several writes, `tx()`, and `batch`. With synchronous commit off, the post-commit `pg_current_wal_lsn()` was behind the post-commit insert LSN on that sample. A paused replica, and both paused, fall back with `fallback:behind` and the row is the one just written. A seeded interleaving of writes, pauses, resumes, and reads had zero missing rows. The cached replay LSN only moves forward. A position read that fails after commit, and a role without execute on the WAL functions, both keep a paused replica off automatic reads (`fallback:position-unknown`) and make a required replica OKM1843. An idle primary with `maxLag: "500ms"` still serves a caught-up replica. Selection on the same topology: weighted shares, `leastConnections` under `pg_sleep`, `latencyAware` through an 80 ms delay on replica b, `maxLag` of `4096B` and `500ms` on paused replay, circuit open and close when a replica container stops and starts, replica failure under a find loop, a custom `select`, and primary fallback. A run of identical characters compresses, so the byte-lag case uses 12,000 random bytes. CI measurements, 40 inserts after 3 warmup, extra cost as the difference of the two samples' percentiles. Postgres 15: p50 0.24 ms (session 0.92, eventual 0.68), p95 0.36 ms (session 1.16, eventual 0.80). Postgres 18: p50 0.19 ms (session 0.90, eventual 0.71), p95 -0.48 ms (session 0.98, eventual 1.46); the negative p95 is one slow eventual insert. Fallback rate with consistency session, one writer of 20 inserts and two find loops, replicas replaying: Postgres 15 was 0 of 24 in 29 ms, Postgres 18 was 0 of 27 in 30 ms. A local sample was 1 of 24. Replica share was 64 of 64 reads on both majors, 8 rounds of 1 write and 8 reads. Smooth weighted round-robin now sets `current` to 0 for a replica that did not take part in the pick. Weights 5 and 1, one pick with only the heavier replica eligible, then 24 reads: before `baaaaabaaaaabaaaaabaaaaa` (first is the lighter), after `abaaaaabaaaaabaaaaabaaaa` (first is the heavier). Both windows are 20 and 4. The classifier is unchanged. A `select` that calls `nextval`, including a view with that body, is a read and fails on the standby. Raw SQL in M2 needs an explicit class. The fix is only in `src/runtime/topology.ts`. Startup minified bytes are unchanged from D204. Gzip is unchanged on this sample: runtime entry 5,288 / 2,026 (224 gzip left under 2,250); featureless app 89,751 / 29,801 (199 left under 30,000); postgres.js 41,137 / 14,502 (98 left under 14,600); PGlite 39,135 / 13,889 (91 left under 13,980); node-postgres 41,547 / 14,671 (249 left under 14,920); Bun.sql 40,395 / 14,183 (287 left under 14,470). Each graph keeps at least 60 gzip bytes. The lazy topology chunk is 21,379 minified and 6,689 gzip (was 21,194 / 6,576). `query-200` stays 17,087 instantiations and 6,280 types. No gate, cap, or ceiling moves. Found in P64. Issue #89
