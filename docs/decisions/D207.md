# D207 — P65: the reference app is the end-to-end proof, and two defects it found are fixed

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

`packages/reference-app` is a private workspace package, a project tracker on public entry points only: workspaces as column-tenancy tenants, members, projects and tasks (`timestamps()`, `archivable()` with a cascade to tasks), comments, a `task_status` enum, the `task_is_open` function, the `active_projects` and `open_tasks` views, the managed `ref_app` role with default grants, and three migrations from `okm generate` (initial, an expand with a batched backfill, a contract). Its schema is the one source: the P65A fixture and its test are removed. The suite runs in the existing `postgres` jobs (15 and 18 on a pull request) and the `tarball` job, with no new check names. **What it proves:** every use case on real Postgres through `okmodel/testing`, the board in one statement and the report in two (`expectQueries`), `isolation()` on every tenant table; two preview jobs at once, each with a database provisioned from the head snapshot and sharing no rows; a rehearsal on a `CREATE DATABASE ... TEMPLATE` clone of a populated database (`okm migrate check`, then expand, backfill, and contract), with the original unchanged and refused by `okm migrate check` as a protected target; reads on a replica, read-your-write with replay paused on both replicas, the report on a replica (`route: "replica"`); a typecheck against the declarations `okmodel` resolves to, including the packed tarball. **What it does not prove:** a UI, a server, load, or soak; schema-per-tenant and database-per-tenant (M5). A read-only reporting role is a deliberate non-goal: fine-grained grants are M2, so the app uses the managed application role only. The role's password and its `SELECT` on `okm_meta` are infrastructure steps (known limit). **Fixes:** (1) two applies on databases of one cluster both found a new managed role absent and every apply but the first failed on `pg_authid_rolname_index` with a raw driver error; on main, 20 of 20 rounds of four parallel provisions failed (60 of 80 applies). `createRoleOnce` in `src/dialects/pg/role/sql.ts` runs the plan's `CREATE ROLE` in a `DO` block that ignores `duplicate_object` and `unique_violation`. Its callers are the only two places that execute `CREATE ROLE`: the step runner (`runUnit` in `src/tooling/migrate/runner.ts`, which also creates the migration role and serves `migrate apply` and `migrate check`'s replay) and the snapshot install (`installSnapshot` in `src/tooling/migrate/snapshot.ts`, which serves an empty-target apply, `migrate check --provision`, and `provision()`). `okmodel/testing` renders `schema.catalog`, which has no roles. The `pg_roles` check stays the fast path and later `ALTER ROLE` steps still run. (2) `schema()` puts views in the query model, so `isolation()` tried to insert into a tenant view and failed with OKM1120; `readFacts` now skips entries with no table object in the catalog. A view that hides the tenant key stays OKM1820 in `okm check`; a read-only isolation check for views is an M2 follow-up. **Cost:** the runtime entry, the featureless app, the four connect entries, and `okmodel/pg` are byte-identical to main; `okmodel/testing` is +103 minified and +30 gzip (139,951 / 44,959), which `scripts/size.ts` reports and does not gate; type probes are unchanged. **Found and left as known limits:** two concurrent `ALTER ROLE` steps on one role fail with `tuple concurrently updated`; on a protected target a migration with a backfill stops after its expand steps (OKM1850 at the backfill, resumable with `--allow-protected`); the expand needs a reviewed `-- okm-allow OKM1531` on its own temporary not-null check (`docs/linter.md`), and the contract a reviewed OKM1512
