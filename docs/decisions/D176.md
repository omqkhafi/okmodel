# D176 — P27 stops at +1,425 / +431; one redesign; findings routed; probe policy

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** dialects
- **Supersedes:** none

---

The first P27 build measured 88,833 / 29,432 for the featureless startup graph, +1,425 minified and +431 gzip over 87,408 / 29,001, against a share of 1,200 / 330 (225 / 101 over) and over the app gate (87,900 / 29,210). Shared core chunk +1,322: `lazyRead` plus `page` and `aggregate` methods in `client.ts` 591, `manyThrough` loading in `plan.ts` 463, `needThrough` calls in `planners()`, `loadWrite` and `loadArchive` 181, `Keyset` and `build` hooks 117; entry +103 (the `manyThrough` branch in `resolveRelations`); lazy chunks `aggregate` 3,974, `page` 3,502, `through` 613 are not in startup. Decision: one redesign, in which the `manyThrough` relation object carries its own emitter and the planner passes its helpers in as arguments (layering holds), which removes about 480 and puts the total near +950; no second redesign, and the stop line stays 1,200 / 330. If it lands and passes the app gate, the gate moves under D160 (measured +3%, never above the 91,000 / 30,000 cap, so it will sit at or just under the cap and leave no slack), and P28 and P29 (600 / 180, 1,400 / 400) then fit only on their stop lines; a cap decision with Ali may be needed at P29 or P30. Query probe: the gated `query-200` is now 16,919 instantiations and 6,219 types because the validation surface moved to augmentation (D174), below the old 17,300 / 7,100, so the one-time raise to 18,000 / 7,400 authorised in D175 is not used and the gated ceilings return to 17,300 / 7,100. The gated probe no longer exercises the features, and an ungated cost is a blind spot: feature rows measured `query-200-validate` 24,885 / 6,412, `page` 18,785 / 6,290, `page` and `aggregate` 20,894 / 6,575, with `manyThrough` 24,049 / 6,729 (each `page` call about +1.9K and `aggregate` about +2.1K instantiations; `manyThrough` +24 / +28 over `many`). Decision: the feature-using probes are gated with their own ceilings at the measured final result plus 3%, rounded down, reported old and new. Findings routed: (1) aggregates decode with the source column's codec, not as `number`: `sum` and `avg` of a numeric column follow the numeric codec, `count` stays a number; done in P27. (2) The client rejects any object value on insert (OKM1121), including Temporal dates, which Cursor reports is the default timestamp codec, and an array insert requires every key even when typed `| undefined`: both are input bugs to verify and fix in a small P27b PR on real Postgres before 0.2, not in P27. (3) `iStartsWith`, `iContains`, `iEndsWith` are in section 12 but not implemented; they are not in 0.2, section 12 says so, and `ilike()` with an escaped pattern is the way until 0.3. (4) The `through` chunk loading on the first operation of any schema that declares `manyThrough` is accepted. Found in P27
