# D172 — P26 stops at +3,402 / +1,077; validation redesign and the real limit

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

The first validation build measured 90,550 / 30,015 for the featureless startup graph, +3,402 minified and +1,077 gzip over the P25 result (87,148 / 28,938), against a share of 500 / 150 (D170). It is also over the app gate (87,900 / 29,210) and 15 gzip bytes over the 91,000 / 30,000 cap. By chunk: shared chunk (client bind, column `.validate`) +923 / +314, entry (schema packing, OKM1030) +2,479 / +773. The engine itself is a separate lazy chunk of 8,025 / 3,005 and is not in the startup total; the write chunk grew 495 / 203. A validating app measures 90,953 / 30,198 at startup, so the cost is paid by apps that never enable validation, which is the thing D163 and D165 forbid. The featureless total graph rose from 139,805 / 45,280 to 151,727 / 49,055 because `client.ts` always holds the dynamic import of the engine. Decision: one redesign, no cap change yet. (1) OKM1030 is a check-time diagnostic (spec: "types + `okm check`"), not a runtime schema step; it leaves the entry chunk. (2) `.validate()` on a column and `validate:` on a table only store their rules as data; nothing is packed or interpreted in core. (3) Everything except one small loader moves into the engine chunk or the `v` module; the client gets `insert.validate`, `check`, `pick`, `omit` through the shared `hook` (D171), not from core. (4) The loader is the only static reference to the engine, so a no-validation app's total graph does not include it. Target: within the D170 share of 500 / 150 over P25. Hard limit: the current gate, which is +752 / +272 over P25; no gate or cap moves in this step. If the redesign is over the gate it stops again with numbers and the question goes to Ali. If it lands, a size audit step (as P23b was, D164) is decided before P27, because P27 to P29 have 3,200 / 910 of shares left and the featureless app is at the cap. Found in P26
