# D122 — Protection per physical database

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

Protection belongs to the physical database. Under `schemaPerTenant` all tenants share one database so protection must be uniform (mixed gives OKM1852); per-tenant protection requires `databasePerTenant`. Targets that resolve to the same host/port/database must have the same protection; two unprotected targets may share a database. Amends D108. Reason: M0-58, M0-59, M0-63
