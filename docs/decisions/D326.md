# D326 — `computed`: relation aggregates in the read statement

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

Approved by Ali on 2026-11-20. Query-level option `computed` on `find`, `one`, `.stream()`, `count`, `exists`, and `page`: a name to a synchronous callback of the row, whose `many` and `manyThrough` relations hold `count({ where, distinct })`, `exists({ where })`, and `sum`, `avg`, `min`, `max` with a field or a `fragment` over the related row's fields. The value is a new key on the row, typed from the verb, and usable in `where` (all six reads) and `orderBy` (`find`, `one`, `.stream()`). The table-level `computed` option keeps its refusal. No new public option and no new `ErrorKind`. Refusals reuse OKM1120, OKM1124, and OKM1125: a name equal to a field, a relation, or another computed name; a `one` or morph relation; a hidden field in any verb or `where`; an async callback or a result that is not an aggregate or a fragment; `page()` ordered by a computed value; `computed` inside `include`; `computed` with a row lock, `distinct`, `distinctOn`, or a window helper; a verb outside the list. SQL: one statement. On `pg` the parent page is a `MATERIALIZED` CTE and each relation is one grouped subquery left-joined to it (`FILTER` for a different `where`, `bool_or` for `exists`, `distinct` inside `count`). A single-column key reads as `= any (array(select ... from page))`, because a semi-join made Postgres run one bitmap scan per parent (2.3 to 3x two queries at 100k parents and 500 related rows with an index). A threshold on a count or an `exists` in `where` becomes `EXISTS (... OFFSET k-1 LIMIT 1)` or `NOT EXISTS` in the page CTE. Every other comparison and an order by a computed value use the full grouped form. Never a correlated subquery per parent and never `LATERAL`. `.stream()` reads its batches in one `REPEATABLE READ` snapshot and needs interactive transactions (OKM1111). The PG words are small named emit points in `src/dialects/pg/computed-sql.ts`. OKM2012 `unindexed-computed-key` (hint in development, never blocks): the related table has no declared index that starts with the foreign key (a primary key, a unique constraint, or a tenant-key-first index count; `manyThrough` is not checked). It prints the `index(...)` line to add. Size, measured on `ad0ed16`: the compiler is the lazy chunk `src/runtime/computed.ts`, loaded from `window.attach`, the hook that already loads for a window helper in `select` (`readHandle` keeps one branch, `lazyBuild`). App startup 94,620 min and 30,787 gzip against 94,559 and 30,752 (+61 and +35), under the gates 94,680 and 30,850. The startup graph gains the option name in the planner key lists, one `bare` flag on `emitWhere`, and that condition. The chunk is 14,995 min and 5,836 gzip, under the 16,000 and 6,500 stop line. `scripts/size.ts` keeps no per-chunk ceiling, and none was added. The tarball is 1,195,042 bytes against 1,173,406 (+21,636), 977 files against 971. No gate moves. Benchmark, not committed: a page of 25 parents with a count and a sum, p50 of 30 warm runs, against the two-query baseline (`find`, then `aggregate` with `groupBy` over the page's ids), Postgres 17.11 and 18.7. With an index on the foreign key the ratio is 0.67 to 1.96 (worst: 17, 100k parents, 500 related rows, 7.87 against 4.02 ms; repeats gave 1.84 and 1.58). Without it, 1.84 to 2.37. Thresholds were 2x with the index and 3x without. Known limits: only `many` and `manyThrough`; no verb that returns a list of related rows; no free table; no `computed` inside `include`; no stored values; `page()` cannot be ordered by a computed value; a parent with tens of thousands of related rows costs about one pass over them.
