# D353 — PostgreSQL 19 is tested; support stays 15 and later

- **Status:** accepted
- **Area:** product
- **Supersedes:** none

---

Tested majors are 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19. Support is open-ended: PostgreSQL 15 and later. A newer major is untested until it is added to that list. `POSTGRES_VERSION` still selects one major, and another integer major runs with a one-line note. No public API change. No new `ErrorKind`. Issue: none (PR only)
