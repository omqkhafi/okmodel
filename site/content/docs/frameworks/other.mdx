---
title: "Other frameworks"
description: "What you need when there is no dedicated example yet."
---
OKModel does not claim tested support for a framework until an example in this repository boots in CI. For everything else, you need:

1. A **supported runtime**: Node.js 22.18+ or Bun 1.4+. Deno is unsupported (D297).
2. A **driver** for that runtime: postgres.js, node-postgres, Bun.sql, PGlite, or Neon. See [Drivers](/docs/operate/drivers).

Wire a process-scoped client on long-lived servers. On isolates (Workers), open and close per request. Keep migrate on a host that can reach Postgres over TCP.

There is no D1 adapter. Do not treat an unlisted framework as CI-verified.
