OKModel talks to Postgres 15, 16, 17, and 18. `connect` refuses an older server.

Pick the driver the same way you pick the client. The calls on `db` do not change.

| Driver | Import | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| postgres.js | `okmodel/pg/postgresjs` | The usual Node and Bun client. |
| node-postgres | `okmodel/pg/pg` | The `pg` package. It does not describe a statement without running it. |
| PGlite | `okmodel/pg/pglite` | Postgres in the same process. No server to start. |
| Bun.sql | `okmodel/pg/bun` | Bun only. It does not describe a statement without running it and does not surface `RAISE NOTICE`. A positive `timeout` on a call, on `tx()`, or on `batch()` is OKM1120 (kind `invalid`). PGlite is the same. A `signal` is not refused, and neither driver cancels a statement already in flight. |

The [compatibility table](/docs/reference/compatibility) is the full list, generated from the conformance run. Install starts on postgres.js and switches the package: `postgres`, `pg`, `@electric-sql/pglite`, or okmodel alone for Bun.sql.
